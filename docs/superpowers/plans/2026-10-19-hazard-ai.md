# Hazard-aware AI — plan

**Goal:** drones and the balance bot shelter from solar flares and keep out of comet paths.
**Architecture:** pure predicates in `sim/hazards.ts`; `pilot.droneThink` and `scripts/bot.ts` call them. Live
comets are collected once per tick in `SimIndex.comets`.
**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest.
**Spec:** [`../specs/2026-10-19-hazard-ai-design.md`](../specs/2026-10-19-hazard-ai-design.md)

## Global Constraints

- `src/sim` stays pure and deterministic: no `Math.random`, no `Date`, no RNG draws in the new code.
- Hazards off is byte-identical: `npm run balance -- --seeds 10 --warps 10` matches `docs/balance.md` apart from
  the header commit hash.
- No save-shape change (no `SAVE_VERSION` bump); no new dependencies; `scratch.md` untouched.
- `npm run check` green after every task.

## Review Focus

- A filter or reflex that fires with hazards off (Task 1 tests the predicates are false with nothing pending).
- Boundary oscillation: a ship that flees must stop outside the trigger zone (Task 1, `flareShelter` flee point).
- Bot orders that undock the flagship mid-flare, e.g. a purchase cycle followed by a fresh `mine` (Task 3).
